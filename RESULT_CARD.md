**Verdict:** The difference in Sharpe (**-0.015**, 95% paired circular block bootstrap interval **[-0.510, +0.511]**) is **not distinguishable from zero**. The rule mainly changes **risk and drawdown behaviour** (cutting annualized volatility by 6.9 percentage points and halving drawdown in the 2025 validation year from -30.57% to -16.30%), rather than adding excess return. Survives start dates: **mixed / regime-dependent** (trails in the 2023–2024 bull run, outperforms on Sharpe and drawdown in 2025–2026). Neighbours ($L \in \{60, 80, 100, 120, 140\}$): **plateau** (net Sharpe smoothly spans 1.108 to 1.447, no isolated spike). Per R8, we do **not** adopt this rule as an alpha generator; it is strictly a volatility/drawdown guardrail.
